The story in three acts

2000–2015
Foundations

AI was a research line item and drones were military or hobby hardware. Then AlexNet won ImageNet in 2012 and the field stopped assuming it had hit a wall.

2016–2025
The inflection

Transformers, then ChatGPT, turned models into products. Drones became both a consumer commodity and the defining weapon of a European war. AI spend alone goes $120B → $410B in these ten years.

2026–2100
Autonomy

The scenario: AI drones outspend conventional ones in 2027, conventional drones fade to a niche, and AI growth slows to a plateau rather than compounding forever.

Where the money actually goes today

  • DeliveryAmazon Prime Air, DoorDash × Wing and Walmart × Wing fly groceries and takeaway to the door in about 30 minutes.
  • HealthZipline has parachuted blood and vaccines into clinics in Rwanda since 2016; Matternet moves lab samples between Swiss hospitals.
  • FarmingSpraying and crop-scouting drones cover huge acreage with less chemical. Low margins plus big fields is exactly where the economics work.
  • DefenceCheap FPV reconnaissance drones became the most consequential weapon of a modern war, and budgets everywhere were replanned around that.
  • IndustryBridges, turbines and pipelines inspected without scaffolding or rope access. The unglamorous, profitable core of the industry.
  • On the groundWaymo robotaxis run the same perception-and-decide stack that flies a drone. The skills transfer between them.

Alberta, briefly

  • Amii, Edmonton2002One of Canada’s three national AI institutes, founded as the Alberta Ingenuity Centre for Machine Learning. Richard Sutton’s reinforcement-learning group made Edmonton a serious AI city long before AI was a household word.
  • Foremost UAS Test Range2015Over 700 square nautical miles of restricted prairie airspace, used for beyond-visual-line-of-sight testing. Alberta’s biggest physical asset in civilian drone work.
  • Meta data centre, Sturgeon County2026More than C$13 billion for a 1 GW campus, Meta’s first in Canada. The pitch is simple: cheap, reliable power and land for AI compute.
